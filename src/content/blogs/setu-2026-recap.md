# Highlights & Key Takeaways from SETU 2026

SETU 2026 brought together over 100 students, industry leaders, and alumni for three days of career mentorship, technical insights, and recruitment opportunities at IOE Pulchowk Campus.

### Day 1: Electrical Engineering Career Prospectus Panel

The conclave opened with an engaging panel discussion focusing on career pathways in utility-scale power systems, renewable energy consultancy, and government service in Nepal. Esteemed speakers shared practical advice on navigating career choices between local opportunities and higher studies abroad.

### Day 2: CV Clinics & Industry Mentorship

Industry expert **Mr. Samay Khadka** led a hands-on session on resume optimization and LinkedIn personal branding. Former NEA Managing Director **Mr. Kulman Ghising** inspired attendees with his keynote address on engineering leadership in Nepal.

### Day 3: Placement Drive

The event concluded with an on-campus placement drive bringing leading technology, power, and engineering firms directly to campus for student interviews.

Thank you to all organizers, committee members, and participants who made SETU 2026 an extraordinary success!
