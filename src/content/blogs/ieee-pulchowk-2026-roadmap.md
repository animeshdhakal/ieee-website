# IEEE Pulchowk 2026: Roadmap & Community Vision

As we embark on the 2026 tenure year, IEEE Pulchowk Student Branch is dedicated to advancing technology for humanity through hands-on technical workshops, research mentorship, hackathons, and international engineering competitions.

### Key Focus Areas for 2026

1. **R&D & Research Mentorship**: Bridging academic projects with publication-ready research through structured workshops on LaTeX, literature review, and academic writing.
2. **Blockchain & Web3 Innovation**: Launching dedicated tracks for decentralized technologies, smart contracts, and legal frameworks in Nepal.
3. **Professional Development & Placement Drives**: Hosting skill-building clinics and placement drives connecting top graduates with industry employers.
4. **Inclusive STEM Initiatives**: Expanding outreach and empowerment through events like *Stemfluence*.

We invite all engineering and technology students at IOE Pulchowk Campus to join our committees and participate in our upcoming events!
