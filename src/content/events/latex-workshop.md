The **LaTeX Workshop** was a hit! Over three engaging days (29 June–1 July), participants mastered document structuring, advanced formatting, and publication-ready typesetting using LaTeX tools and templates.

Our attendees gained hands-on experience from crafting professional papers to custom page layouts. The workshop covered:
- **Document Structuring**: Organizing content into logical sections and subsections.
- **Advanced Formatting**: Managing complex layouts, tables, and mathematical equations.
- **Publication-ready Typesetting**: Using LaTeX templates to create research papers that meet academic standards.

Big thanks to **Our Mentors**, our partners, and everyone who joined for making it possible. Let’s keep writing beautifully formatted research! Stay tuned for more workshops!
