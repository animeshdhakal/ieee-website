# SETU 2026

## Day 1: Panel Discussion
**Theme:** Prospectus of Electrical Engineers in Government and Private Sectors

![SETU 2026 Day 1 Photo 2](/gallery/setu-2026-day1-2.webp)
![SETU 2026 Day 1 Photo 3](/gallery/setu-2026-day1-3.webp)
![SETU 2026 Day 1 Photo 4](/gallery/setu-2026-day1-4.webp)

The first day kicked off with an insightful panel discussion attended by 57 participants. Moderated by Grishma Sitaula and Princy Mishra, the panel featured esteemed guest speakers:
- **Subrat Aryal** (Deputy Manager at Nepal Electricity Authority)
- **Saroj Koirala** (Founder and Principal Consultant at Mrigasheera Consulting & Training Services)
- **Kushal Gautam** (Founder and Managing Director at Quasar Energy Consultants)

### Main Topics Discussed
- Prospectus of Electrical Engineers in Government and Private Sectors in Nepal
- Technical skill requirements for utility-scale power sector work
- Career pathways in renewable energy consultancy and climate finance
- Entrepreneurship in the engineering sector: when and how to take the leap
- Multi-disciplinary qualifications and their value in the energy industry
- Nepal vs. abroad: where to build a career as an EE graduate
- Smart grids, energy storage, and emerging roles in Nepal’s power sector
- Communication, stakeholder management, and soft skills in engineering careers

### Key Insights
**Subrat Aryal** shared valuable insights about working at the Nepal Electricity Authority (NEA) and pursuing a career in the government sector. He encouraged students to explore opportunities in government service and emphasized the significant role engineers can play in national development. He also encouraged students to think of life as a closed-loop control system, continuously measuring output, comparing it with the desired outcome, and adjusting accordingly.

**Kushal Gautam** inspired the attendees by highlighting that engineering is not just about machines—it is about people. He emphasized that what sets great engineers apart is their ability to adapt, learn quickly, and solve problems in unfamiliar situations.

**Saroj Koirala** emphasized the importance of staying curious, being open to learning new things, and exploring areas beyond one's core field. He shared his perspective on the subjective decision of staying in Nepal versus going abroad, advising students to make the best decision with the available information and commit to making the most of their chosen path.

### Major Concerns Raised by Students
- Whether to pursue higher education in Nepal or seek opportunities abroad.
- Government sector vs. private sector: long-term growth, job satisfaction, and financial security.
- Career growth timelines in Nepal’s energy sector.
- Qualities, soft skills, and extracurricular experiences employers actively look for.
- Early steps toward consultancy or entrepreneurship.
- Opportunities in climate finance and green energy for Nepali graduates.

## Day 2: Clinics and Guest Speakers

Day 2 began with a **CV Building and LinkedIn Clinic** by **Mr. Samay Khadka**, CEO of Sharing Opportunities. He provided clear and generous guidance on how to present oneself to the world, walking students through the gaps in their resumes and reminding them that a well-told story on paper can open doors.

Following this, **Mr. Kulman Ghising**, the Former Managing Director of Nepal Electricity Authority, graced the event. He shared honest insights and challenged the students to think bigger about what engineers in Nepal can achieve.

The day concluded with **Mock Interview Training** led by **Mr. Rohini Paudel**, Deputy Manager at Nepal Electricity Authority. The session served as a mirror, showing students where they currently stand and where they need to grow.

Day 2 reminded everyone why this conclave was built: not just to fill a schedule, but to give every student a clearer sense of direction.

## Day 3: Placement Drive

Day 3 focused entirely on career advancement. Placement companies from various sectors—including tech, power & energy, construction, and design—visited the campus to conduct interviews and recruit promising candidates.
