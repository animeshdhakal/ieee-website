# Digital Forensics: A Practical Cybersecurity Application

**Duration:** 3-Day Hands-on Workshop  
**Location:** IOE Pulchowk Campus  
**Organized by:** IEEE Pulchowk Student Branch  

The **Hands-on Digital Forensics Workshop** was conducted over three action-packed days, giving students practical skills in evidence acquisition, filesystem analysis, artifact extraction, and enterprise incident response.

---

### Day 1: Laying the Foundations

A great start to our Hands-on Digital Forensics Workshop!

The Day 1 session introduced participants to the fundamentals of digital forensics through practical, hands-on learning. From exploring key cybersecurity concepts to getting familiar with essential tools, Day 1 laid a strong foundation for the exciting sessions ahead.

A big thank you to our instructor, **Nidan Poudel**, for sharing his knowledge and experience, and to all the participants for their enthusiasm and active engagement.

### Day 2: Practical Evidence Analysis & Live Demos

Day 2 came with fewer theories and a lot more evidence.

From live demonstrations to hands-on learning, the room was filled with curiosity, questions, and plenty of *"wait... that's actually possible?"* moments. Participants explored memory forensics, deleted file carving, and network log reconstruction.

Thank you to our incredible speaker **Dikshya Shrestha** and everyone who joined us in making Day 2 of *Digital Forensics: A Practical Cybersecurity Application* such a success.

### Day 3: Security Operations & Incident Response (SecOps)

Day 3 of our Hands-on Digital Forensics Workshop brought the journey to a meaningful close with an insightful session on **Security Operations and Incident Response**. 

Participants explored how cybersecurity professionals detect, analyse, and respond to real-world threats, gaining a broader perspective on protecting digital systems beyond forensic investigations.

A sincere thank you to our speaker, **Aashish Karki**, Associate SecOps Engineer at Guardsix, for sharing his expertise and industry experience. We also extend our gratitude to all the participants for their enthusiasm, curiosity, and active involvement throughout the three-day workshop.
