# Paper Reading Workshop: "Attention Is All You Need"

**Date:** July 24, 2026  
**Location:** IOE Pulchowk Campus  
**Organized by:** IEEE Pulchowk Student Branch  

The **Paper Reading Workshop** on the seminal paper *"Attention Is All You Need"* provided participants with valuable insights into understanding research papers, dissecting complex technical concepts, and strengthening analytical thinking in machine learning and natural language processing.

Participants explored the foundational architecture of the Transformer model, the mathematics behind self-attention mechanisms, and best practices for critically analyzing breakthrough research publications.

---

### Highlights of the Session

- **Decoding the Transformer Architecture**: Understanding multi-head self-attention, positional encodings, and feed-forward networks.
- **Reading Research Critically**: Developing structured frameworks to review methodologies, claims, and ablation studies in modern AI papers.
- **Interactive Discussion**: Collaborative breakout conversations exploring subsequent advancements (BERT, GPT, and modern Large Language Models).
