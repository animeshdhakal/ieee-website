A remarkable chapter unfolded at the **IEEE ACEI StartUp Nepal (The National Pitstop to the Regionals)**, proudly backed by IEEE Region 10 and the IEEE Pulchowk and KEC student branches!

Ten passionate teams poured their hearts into their ideas, transforming competition into a vibrant celebration of creativity, support, and innovation. Beyond the pitches, friendships blossomed, minds expanded, and everyone walked away inspired and hopeful. Together, we proved that the real win is learning from each other and dreaming bigger, side by side.

The winning three teams will proceed to the semifinals and then to the regionals, after which the next stop will be **GITEX Global in Dubai**. The top 10 teams will be incubated by **Idea Studio Nepal**.

A huge thank you to all our volunteers, participants, judges, and everyone who contributed—this event truly wouldn’t have been the same without your dedication and passion. It’s the shared commitment, compassion, and collaboration that made this experience so incredibly meaningful!
