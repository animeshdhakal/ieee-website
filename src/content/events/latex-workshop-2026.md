# LaTeX Workshop 2026

**Dates:** 12–14 August 2026  
**Time:** 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM  
**Venue:** IOE Pulchowk Campus  
**Instructor:** Mr. Bipul Mainali  

The **LaTeX Workshop 2026**, organized by the **IEEE Pulchowk Student Branch**, was successfully conducted over three intensive, hands-on morning sessions from August 12 to August 14, 2026.

Designed specifically for engineering and technical students, the workshop took participants through the essentials of LaTeX typesetting for academic and research workflows.

---

### Key Highlights & Curriculum

- **Introduction to LaTeX & Environment Setup**: Getting started with TeXstudio, document structures, packages, and preamble configuration.
- **Document Structuring & Formatting**: Chapters, sections, custom page geometries, headers, footers, and margins.
- **Mathematical Typesetting**: Writing complex formulas, equations, matrices, and symbols with `amsmath` and `amssymb`.
- **Tables, Figures & Cross-Referencing**: Clean table design, image placement, floating environments, and automated label references.
- **Bibliography & Citations**: Managing references and citation styles using BibTeX for academic publications.
- **Project Report & Thesis Templates**: Real-world templates tailored for IOE Pulchowk Campus engineering project reports.
