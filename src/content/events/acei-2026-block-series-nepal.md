# ACEI 2026 – The Venture & Block-Series Nepal

**Dates:** July 26 – August 2, 2026  
**Venue:** Leapfrog Technology & Pulchowk Campus  
**Organized by:** IEEE Pulchowk Student Branch  

From insightful sessions and engaging discussions to inspiring startup pitches, **ACEI 2026 – The Venture** celebrated innovation, entrepreneurship, and collaboration at every step. 

The **Final Pitching Day** brought together passionate innovators, industry experts, and aspiring entrepreneurs, creating a vibrant platform where ideas were shared, challenged, and transformed into real-world possibilities.

---

### Highlights & The Journey

- **Block-Series Nepal Symposium**: Exploring the legal, regulatory, and practical horizons of blockchain technology in Nepal with legal and industry leaders.
- **Idea2Startup Acceleration**: Intensive mentoring sessions refining business models, unit economics, and technical roadmaps.
- **Final Pitch Day**: High-stakes startup presentations judged by prominent venture mentors and tech leaders.
- **Winner Felicitation**: Awarding top teams with cash prizes, incubation pathways, and certificates.
